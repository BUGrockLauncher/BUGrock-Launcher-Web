Engineered For Freedom

Built for True Minecraft Bedrock Players

BUGrock Launcher eliminates the store restrictions, allows multi-version staging, and offers deep control.

Side-by-Side Installations

Download Release, Preview, and Beta builds together. Switch between them without reinstalling or losing your single-player worlds.

GDK (.msixvc) & UWP (.appx)

Seamless support for modern GDK (1.21+) packages with automatic decryption key verification and legacy UWP sideloading.

Offline Package Sideloading

Import local .msixvc and .appx packages directly from your drive. Play archived updates offline without re-downloading from store servers.

World Preservation & Conflict Alert

Safeguards your saves from being wiped by Windows when uninstalling UWP packages via temporary safe-holding, and alerts you if worlds exist across both UWP and GDK directories with an interactive conflict dialog.

Instant 1-Click Version Switcher

Switch between your installed Release, Preview, and Beta versions right from the main Play dashboard with immediate package registration.

100% Free & Transparent
